TRAFFIC SAFETY RULES

Wrong-Way Driving:
Vehicles should travel in the permitted direction of traffic.
Wrong-way movement can create a serious collision risk.

Traffic Congestion:
High vehicle density can reduce traffic flow and increase
the possibility of collisions.

Pedestrian Safety:
Vehicles should give appropriate consideration to pedestrians
at crossings and designated pedestrian areas.

Restricted Areas:
Vehicles should not enter areas marked as restricted unless
authorized.

Emergency Response:
When a potentially dangerous traffic event is detected,
the event should be flagged for human review.
